The game

Experteleven is an online game. Matches are simulated on our server, and every club in your league is run by another player or by the computer. You need an account to play. You can have one account and manage one club.

Fair play

  • Don't use several accounts to move players or money between clubs.
  • Don't use bots, scripts or bugs to get an advantage. Tell us about bugs instead.
  • Choose manager and club names that aren't offensive, hateful or someone else's trademark.

We may rename clubs, undo unfair transfers, or close accounts that break these rules.

Manager Pro

Manager Pro is an optional monthly subscription sold through Google Play. It adds club colours and a crest, a richer club and manager history, and match replays. It does not affect results, players or money. It renews every month until you cancel it in Google Play; cancelling stops the next renewal and Pro stays active to the end of the paid month. Refunds follow Google Play's rules and your consumer rights.

The service

We work to keep the game running, but it may sometimes be unavailable, and we may change features, balance or the rules of the game. Game items such as players, clubs and in-game money have no real-world value and can't be sold or exchanged for money.

Ending

You can delete your account at any time (how). If we ever shut the game down, we will tell players in the app at least 30 days before.
