Delete your Experteleven account

Experteleven: Football Manager · mrbooz AB

In the app

  1. Open Experteleven and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account and data.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm with your password.

Deletion happens at once. If you want a copy of your data first, tap Export data on the same screen.

Without the app

Email hello@mrbooz.com from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We do it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your login, email address and password hash.
  • Your manager name and nationality (the manager is shown as "Former manager").
  • Your club's inbox and any open transfer offers.
  • The IP addresses in our security log.

What stays

Your club stays in its league, run by the computer, so other players' fixtures still work. Its results, players and transfer history stay in the game without any link to you. A Manager Pro subscription is handled by Google Play: cancel it in the Play Store, since deleting your account doesn't cancel it.
