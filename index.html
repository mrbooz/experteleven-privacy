Privacy Policy

Experteleven: Football Manager · Android · mrbooz AB · Last updated 20 November 2026

Experteleven is an online football manager game made by mrbooz AB, Sweden. mrbooz AB is the controller of your personal data. There are no ads and no analytics or tracking tools in the app.

What we store

  • Account: your email address and a hashed password (never the password itself).
  • Manager: the manager name and nationality you choose.
  • Game data: your club, its name and colours, tactics, transfers, finances, matches and in-game messages.
  • Security log: sign-ins and account changes, with the IP address they came from.
  • Manager Pro: if you subscribe, the product, a purchase token from Google Play and when it renews or ends. We never see your card details.

Why

To run your account and the shared game world, to confirm Manager Pro, and to keep the service secure, for example to stop account abuse and unfair trades between linked accounts. The legal basis is our contract with you (running the game) and our legitimate interest in security.

Who can see it

Your manager name, club name and colours, and your results are visible to other players, because they play in the same world. Your email address is never shown to anyone.

We do not sell or share your data. Our hosting provider stores it on our behalf. Google Play handles payments under Google's privacy policy.

How long

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete it, we delete your login and email address and remove your name from the game straight away. Your club goes back to the computer and keeps playing, so other players' leagues are not affected; its results stay in the history without any link to you.

Your rights

You can download all the data we hold about you (Settings → Account and data → Export data) and delete your account at any time (how to delete your account). You can also ask us to correct or restrict your data, or object to how we use it, by emailing us. You can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Children

Experteleven is not directed at children under 13.

Security

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above. Questions: hello@mrbooz.com · mrbooz AB, Sweden.
